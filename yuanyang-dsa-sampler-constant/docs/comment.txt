Subject: Round1: Public Comment: YuanYang.DSA — wrong sampler constant in the -512 implementation leaks the secret key into the signature mean

Dear NGCC evaluation committee, dear YuanYang.DSA team,

We report an implementation bug in YuanYang.DSA-512 (reference and optimized implementations;
-1024/-2048 are not affected). The specification and the design are not affected; the fix is one
constant and one table. The issue is independent of, and adds to, ngcc.dev sign-34-1.

1. Root cause. The wide integer sampler in the presampler uses an RCDT table for standard deviation
4η (Spec. Table 2; sampler.c "sigma = 4*eta"; L = 4 in sign.c), but its rejection constant
fpr_yuanyang_inv_2sqr_large_sampler_sigma (fpr.h, 0x3ccc24a85, 43-bit fixed point) decodes to
1/(2(16η)²), sixteen times too small; the zero-centred BerExp table was generated from the same
value. The -1024 constants decode to 4η exactly. Consequence: the rejection step almost always
accepts, so the zero-centred sampler returns −z0 or z0+1 with equal probability. Its mean is +0.458
instead of 0 (variance 18.46 instead of 16.84), and the centred sampler largely ignores the
fractional part of its centre.

2. Effect on signatures. The perturbation inherits E[x] = 0.458·𝟙, so the released signature
satisfies E[s] = (0.458/4)·Â·(𝟙,𝟙) + const, where Â is the rounded Gram root stored in the signing
key. Measured on the unmodified reference (two keys, 4·10^5 signatures each, s2 recomputed as the
verifier does): the mean is non-zero at χ² z ≈ 2300 already with 2·10^4 signatures; after removing
the all-ones factor (𝟙·(1−X) = 2), the s1 mean regressed on the true a00 gives slope 0.102 ± 0.011
and 0.113 ± 0.010 (predicted 0.1145), and 0.010 ± 0.010 / −0.011 ± 0.013 against the other key's
a00; the s2 half behaves the same with a10 + a11. The public mean is thus an unbiased estimator of
a00 with coefficient error 432/√N.

3. Impact. With sign-34-1 present, a00·ā00 = σ² − 1 − (f f̄ + g ḡ) exactly, so the transcript
exposes f f̄ + g ḡ, the quantity Fouque–Kirchner–Tibouchi–Wallet–Yu (EUROCRYPT 2020, ePrint
2019/1180) show suffices with the public key for full key recovery. By simulation with the measured
noise, exact recovery of f f̄ + g ḡ by rounding needs about 2^35 signatures: inside the 2^80
signature bound of the specification, but not practical. We have not carried out a key recovery and
do not claim one. The bug also inflates the signature variance by 3.2% and the norm-rejection rate
from about 2% to 15% per signature.

4. Related fix items. (i) The -512 Δ2 constant fpr_yuanyang_inv_2sqrsigma_sig is 2^12 times
1/(2σ_sig²) (the 1024/2048 values are exact); correcting it brings the Δ2 rejection rate to the 1.5%
the specification predicts. Fixing sign-34-1 alone raises Δ2 rejections to 0.4–1.1 per signature,
so the two fixes belong together. (ii) With all three corrections the transcript is isotropic in our
tests (mean z ≤ 1.5, secret-direction variance ratios 1.000 ± 0.001, autocorrelation at noise level)
and 600/600 signatures verify. (iii) Table 3 lists σ_sig = 128.262 for -2048; η·σ = 118.3, matching
the code.

Suggested fix: set the constant to 0x3ccc24a84f and regenerate the zero-centred BerExp table;
correct the -512 Δ2 constant; add a signature-mean/variance self-test to the KAT suite.
