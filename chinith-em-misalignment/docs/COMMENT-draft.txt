Subject: Re: Round1: Public Comment: Chinith -- uBlockith-EM: one-block misalignment in the constraint chain (spec and code)

Dear Chinith team,

Following the September 30 clarification of issues 1-4, we report a further defect in the uBlockith-EM constraint system that those corrections do not cover. It is present in both the June 30 specification and the submitted code; in the specification it also removes the public output from the proven relation. Page numbers are physical PDF pages; code paths are relative to Implementations/Reference_Implementation/ublockith_em_d3_256f/ (256s and both optimized trees are identical at the cited lines).

1. The defect

uBlockith-EM proves y = E_x(k) xor k over the full 24-round uBlock-256 (Table 2, R = 24). The witness is S0 = k, S2, ..., S22 (l_enc = 3072 bits), and EncCstrnts (p.68) checks 12 double-round transitions ending at out = k xor y.

In the EM branch of OWFConstraints (p.69), step 9 takes in = w[0..Nblock-1] and step 17 selects w~ = w[l_ke ..] with l_ke = 0, so w~ already begins with S0 = in (as p.67 states). EncCstrnts step 6 prepends in again:

    w* = in || in || S2 || S4 || ... || S22 || out   (14 blocks)

Every transition is shifted by one block. Iteration j = 0 enforces DoubleRound_0(in) = in, which no honest witness satisfies; j = 1..11 relate prover-chosen blocks only; and at i = R-2 (steps 24-27) the algorithm reads block 12 = S22, not out (the comment on step 26 says "out = k_bar[R] xor (x0, x1)"). Block 13 = out is never read, so pk2 does not enter the relation.

The code has the same prepend: ublockith_ublock_256.c passes in = w[0..32) and also w + lke_bytes (= w) as the encryption witness (prover lines 181/190, verifier 264); ublock_constraints.c:778-784 builds in || in || S2 || ... || S20 || out with ENC_W_STATES = 11, dropping S22. Unlike the specification, the code reads out directly in its "i == UBLOCK_ROUNDS - 2" branch, so pk2 is bound in the code.

2. Consequence A (specification and code): honest signatures are rejected

With the announced issue-1 fix applied (a_tilde_0 hashed into chall_3 in signer and verifier) and nothing else changed, ublockith_em_d3_256s/f reject all of their own signatures (reference 256f 0/5, reference 256s 0/2, optimized 256f 0/3), while the non-EM sets verify 5/5. Shifting the encryption witness by one block (w + 32 bytes; tags/keys + 256 elements) restores 5/5. The submitted KATs pass only because, with a_tilde_0 unbound, the constraint values never reach the verdict.

3. Consequence B (specification as written): the relation collapses to a 2-round fixed point

Since blocks 0 and 1 are the same committed value, the only constraint on in is DoubleRound_0(in) = in under the public round keys derived from pk1; out is never read. A spec-conformant verifier therefore accepts, for any message and any pk2, any fixed point of the first two rounds together with the honestly computed chain. We confirmed this in the clear: for arbitrary x and two unrelated pk2, constraints j = 1..11 hold and only j = 0 fails, by exactly DoubleRound_0(x) xor x.

To quantify the reduced relation we estimated the cost of such a fixed point. Whole-instance SAT on DR0(x) = x does not terminate within hours for random keys (the preimage DR0(x) = c solves in under 2 s, so the encoding is not the bottleneck). A standard guess-and-determine approach does: fixing g bits of in, interleaved nibble-wise across both branches, and SAT-solving the residual, wrong guesses are refuted quickly and 2^g x T_wrong is minimized near g = 108. On a real key, 8 wrong guesses were refuted in 17.1 s on average (max 30.7 s), i.e. about 2^108 x 2^27.5 = 2^135.5 double-round evaluations against the claimed 256-bit level. This is an upper bound obtained in a few hours, extrapolated from measured per-guess cost (the 2^108 enumeration was not run); it is certificational, not practical; it applies to the ~63% of keys whose DoubleRound_0 has a fixed point; and it concerns the specification only, since the code binds pk2 and is affected by Consequence A alone.

4. Suggested fix

Skip S0 when selecting the encryption witness in the EM branch (p.69 step 17: w[Nblock .. l_enc - 1]; code: w + 32 at ublockith_ublock_256.c:190/264), or do not prepend in at EncCstrnts step 6 for EM. Either restores the 24-round chain ending at out. We would also suggest stating the EM witness layout explicitly in the revision (whether l_enc = 3072 includes S0) and re-validating the corrected release end to end with a_tilde_0 bound.
